History of Pakistan

Pakistan has a rich history, including ancient civilizations and the Pakistan Movement. Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947.

Indus Valley Civilization

An ancient civilization known for cities such as Mohenjo-daro.

Pakistan Movement

A political movement that led to the creation of Pakistan.

Independence 1947

Pakistan emerged as an independent country in August 1947.

Badshahi Mosque

Minar-e-Pakistan

A monument associated with the Lahore Resolution.
